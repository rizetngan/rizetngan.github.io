## Repository purpose

The current site is an **interaction-only CSS-3D graybox prototype**, not the approved final visual design. Keep it available as a functional reference, but **do not preserve its look and feel**. Rebuild the visual experience to a refined creative portfolio.

## Brand and positioning

- Brand: **rizet ngan.**
- Concept: **The Product Atelier** — an explorable product-making atelier.
- Identity: product thinker / AI product manager / independent maker. Not a recruitment résumé.
- Mission: show how ideas evolve into tangible software and products.
- Core works: Actape (https://github.com/rizetngan/Actape), Snug for Windows (https://github.com/rizetngan/snug-for-windows). Do not invent business cases or metrics.

## Visual direction (LOCKED)

- Architectural private gallery × working product atelier, with an intimate human touch.
- Warm architectural minimalism; calm and precise.
- Medium-strength **Precision Sketch → Material Reality** visual transformation. Corridor and important objects begin as precise pencil/architectural linework and reveal tactile material as people approach; reveal is part of interaction, not a perpetual screen-wide shader. Preserve explored state subtly.
- Colors: ivory #F2F0EB, ink #202321, terracotta #A04D3E, stone #E4E0D7, walnut #8E715F, sketch graphite #77756F.
- Materials: warm plaster, limestone/microcement, smoked wood, muted graphite metal, tactile paper. Soft daylight.
- Typography: serif sparingly for the logo/hero, precise sans-serif for navigation, readable Chinese typography.
- **Do not** create a cartoon sketch game, neon sci-fi room, generic SaaS dashboard, luxury hotel corridor, or overly decorated 3D demo.

## Spatial architecture

1. Threshold: quiet opening foyer with `rizet ngan.` mark and a sketch corridor.
2. Corridor: a meandering gallery spine with architectural thresholds and partially open areas, not four identical hotel doors.
3. Work Archive: finished product artifacts; real screens and case-study pages.
4. Prototype Bench: the emotional center; desk, monitor, sketches, devices, working prototypes.
5. Notes Library: personal thoughts, AI and product notes, legible article reading.
6. About corner: maker story, interests, contact.

## Navigation (LOCKED)

- Default **Guided** experience: mouse click and scroll advance curated camera positions, click hotspots directly, no keyboard required.
- Optional **Free Roam**: press F or click low-profile toggle; WASD and mouse movement, E interact, Esc exit. Preserve approximate place when switching.
- Reading overlays take over scroll and release Pointer Lock. Close restores previous location and mode.
- Always provide ordinary navigation and direct links; no important content solely accessible via 3D.
- Mobile: lightweight guided/tap UI, no forced on-screen joystick; no-WebGL fallback.
